# Mimi — privacy policy

**Last updated: 2026-09-26**

Mimi collects no data.

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
- **No data leaves your browser.** Mimi makes no network requests of its own. It never contacts Cantum, YGG HOMME, or any third party. There is no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
- **Audio stays on your machine.** Pitch and speed are processed inside the browser tab in real time. Nothing is recorded, uploaded, or stored.
- **What is stored, and where.** Your preferences (interface, Mimi's look, default range, engine choice) and, if you leave "Remember settings per video" on, the pitch and speed you chose per video (keyed by the YouTube video id) are saved with Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If you are signed into Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome may sync that storage across your own devices through your Google account — that is Chrome's sync, governed by Google's privacy policy, not a Mimi server. Turn "Remember settings per video" off in Mimi's options to stop per-video memory; uninstalling the extension removes all of it.
- **Permissions.** `storage` (the preferences above) and access to `www.youtube.com` pages (to find the video element and draw the controls on the player). Nothing else.
- **Open source.** The full source is at https://github.com/cantum-io/mimi under the GPL-2.0, so all of the above can be checked.
