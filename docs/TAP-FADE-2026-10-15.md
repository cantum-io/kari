## One root cause, two symptoms

YouTube hides its controls (`ytp-autohide` on `#movie_player`) after ~3 s without a pointer move. The dock stops
clicks, pointer-downs and wheel at its host so they never reach the player, but it never touches `mousemove`, and a
pointer **parked on a pill** sends none. So while Jesse sits on Tap, YouTube counts him idle, `ytp-autohide` lands,
and the dock (by the earlier rule) hides its panel with it: `visibility:hidden`, `pointer-events:none`. That is defect 2.
Defect 1 follows from it: the next click at the same spot goes through the hidden panel to the player, which toggles
**pause**; a paused video has a frozen media clock, every tap after that is a 0 s gap, and the tapper ignores 0 s gaps
as bounces (by design) — nothing changes on the readout. "Sometimes" = whichever tap happened to come after the
3 s idle.

### Fix (dock.ts `kept`, one CSS line)

The panel is now **kept** through YouTube's autohide while it is in use:

- the pointer is inside the panel, the gear or the settings (`pointerenter` / `pointerleave` on those elements);
- anything inside the dock has focus (the bpm field, a tabbed-to button);
- a real click, pointer-down, key or input happened within the last 3 s (`HOLD_MS`, YouTube's own idle figure) — so a
  tap burst followed by moving away still shows its bpm. Synthetic clicks (a MIDI pad, a test) do not count.

Under the autohide `.root[data-kept="1"]{cursor:default}` keeps YouTube's `cursor:none` off the panel. Kari's blob
is unchanged: it never fades, and hovering the blob alone does not keep the panel (moving into the panel does). YouTube's
own control bar is left alone — Kari feeds nothing into YouTube's timer.
